# Fencing Installation Quoting Guide (Wood and Metal) — 2025–2026

Cost ranges for residential fence installation in the United States. Values are in USD and include labor and materials unless stated. Verify local pricing; costs vary by region and site conditions.

## 1  Site-Visit Checklist

- **Fence length and height** — total linear footage; desired height (6 ft vs 8 ft)
- **Type of fence** — material (wood species, vinyl, steel, aluminum, wrought iron) and style (privacy, picket, split-rail)
- **Posts and footings** — wood or metal posts, footing depth, concrete required; check for utility lines
- **Gates** — count pedestrian and driveway gates, widths, hardware preferences
- **Site conditions** — soil type (rocky/clay requires augers), slope, vegetation, tree roots, equipment access; sloped or rocky terrain adds 15–40% to labor
- **Existing fence removal** — removal and disposal costs $10–$20 per linear foot
- **Permits and property lines** — permits often $40–$150; surveys $200–$1,200
- **Add-ons** — lattice tops, decorative post caps, stain/paint, privacy slats, driveway gate automation

### 2.3  Posts and Hardware

- **Wood posts** with concrete footings: add ~$3–5/lf
- **Metal posts** (reduces maintenance): add ~$5–10/lf
- **Post spacing**: standard 6–8 ft; use 8 ft posts for 6 ft fences, 10 ft for 8 ft fences
- **Fasteners**: corrosion-resistant screws/nails $0.50–1.50/lf; stainless steel more expensive

### 2.4  Gates

| Gate Type | Cost |
|---|---|

### 2.5  Finishing and Maintenance

- **Staining / painting**: $2–14/lf; always include to protect wood and prolong life
- **Maintenance cycles**: Pine/spruce — reseal every 1–2 years; Cedar/redwood — every 3–5 years

## 5  Pricing Methodology

### 5.1  Materials

1. **Linear footage × unit cost/ft** for chosen material and height
2. **Posts**: `(lf ÷ spacing) + 1`; standard spacing 6–8 ft
3. **Concrete**: 1 bag/post for 6 ft fences; 2 bags/post for 8 ft fences; ~$5–10/bag
4. **Gates**: add gate cost + hinges/latches/handles ($50–150/gate)
5. **Finish**: stain/paint for wood; powder coat/galvanize for metal if not pre-finished

### 5.2  Labor

- **Crew of two**: 100–150 lf/day for 6 ft wood privacy; less for 8 ft or difficult terrain
- **Wood labor rate**: $30–50/hr
- **Metal labor rate**: $40–60/hr (higher for welding/fabrication)

### 5.3  Overhead and Profit (Cost-Plus)

1. Sum: materials + labor + equipment + site prep + disposal
2. Overhead: +10–15% (insurance, trucks, admin)
3. Profit margin: +10–20%
4. Material markup: 10–20%
5. Contingency for complex sites/uncertain soil: +5–10%

### 5.4  Demolition Options

- Offer as line item: $10–20/lf removal + disposal
- DIY removal by customer: remove line item
- Tree/shrub removal: itemize by count

## 7  Summary

- **Wood privacy fences**: $20–50/lf installed; 8 ft tall → $50–75/lf; species choice drives cost 2–3×
- **Picket / split-rail**: less material, $15–40/lf
- **Metal fences**: $20–85/lf; aluminum $25–75, steel $20–55, wrought iron $50–85
- **Labor**: wood $15–20/lf; metal $5–25/lf; wrought iron higher due to welding
- **Gates**: walk gates $200–600; drive gates $300–800; ornamental wrought iron $800–2,000
- **Slope / rocky terrain**: +15–40% labor
- **Staining/sealing**: $2–14/lf; essential for wood longevity
- **Always**: verify permits, confirm property lines, include contingency for unknown soil
