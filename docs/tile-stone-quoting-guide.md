# Tile and Stone Installation Quoting Guide

## Key Data Collection During the Site Visit

Use a simple checklist during your visit to capture the information you'll need to build an accurate quote:

- **Area measurements** – measure the length and width of each area to be tiled. Note separate measurements for floors, walls, showers, backsplashes or outdoor patios. Record the total square metres.
- **Project location and use** – note whether the installation is indoors (floor, wall, backsplash, shower) or outdoors (patio, walkway, pool deck). Outdoor installations require thicker tiles and weather-rated adhesives, and they typically cost 15–20% more.
- **Substrate condition** – check whether the existing subfloor or surface is level, clean and sound. Note if old tile, linoleum or carpet must be removed. Removal adds about US$2–7 per square metre for floors and US$2–5 per square metre for backsplashes. Subfloor levelling or replacing adds another US$2–5 per square metre.
- **Tile type and size** – identify the material (ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, glass, mosaic, etc.), size (small mosaic, standard 30×30 cm, large format 60×120 cm or larger) and finish. Material prices vary widely: ceramic tile costs roughly US$1–8 per square metre, porcelain US$4–14, natural stone (granite, marble, slate, travertine) US$7–25 and glass US$12–35.
- **Design complexity** – note whether the layout is a simple straight-lay grid or involves patterns (herringbone, chevron, diagonal, custom mosaics). Complex patterns require more cuts, waste and labour. Installing herringbone or chevron increases labour time by 20–30% and waste by 15–20%, adding roughly US$2–4 per square metre.
- **Special features** – record any niches, benches, waterproof membranes, radiant heating mats, decorative borders or trims. Shower benches and niches add US$200–500. Heated floors add US$5–12 per square metre.
- **Access and working conditions** – note obstacles, narrow spaces, stairs or multi-storey access that may slow the job. Record whether work is in an occupied home or new construction.

## Quoting Methodology

**Formula:**
```
Total Price = (Area × Material Cost) + (Area × Labour Cost)
            + (Prep + Demo + Membranes + Features)
            + Overhead + Profit
```

### Step-by-Step Calculation

1. **Calculate areas** – Multiply length × width for each section (floor, wall, backsplash, shower, patio). For showers, measure floors and walls separately.
2. **Select material** – Choose tile type, size and pattern. Input unit material cost from tables. For complex patterns, add $22–$43/m² to labour. For outdoor, add 15–20% to both costs.
3. **Estimate labour hours** – Multiply area by unit labour cost ($54–$215/m² floors; $43–$129/m² showers). Adjust upward for pattern complexity, large tiles, mosaics or difficult access.
4. **Add preparation and features** – Remove old tile, subfloor levelling, cement board, membranes, heated floors, benches, niches as separate line items.
5. **Account for waste** – Add 10–15% extra material for cuts and breakage; 15–20% for complex patterns.
6. **Overhead and profit** – Add a fixed percentage (e.g., 10% overhead + 10–20% profit) on top of labour and material costs.
7. **Present options** – Provide at least two material options (standard and premium) and note the cost difference for custom patterns or heated floors.
