> Reference document for the TTC Estimator painting project types.

## Site Visit & Information Gathering

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|
| **Building type & location** | Residential vs. commercial/institutional; occupied vs. vacant; single vs. multi-story |
| **Interior vs. exterior surfaces** | Identify siding material (wood, vinyl, stucco, brick, metal); note wall heights, vaulted ceilings |
| **Room measurements** | Wall area = length × height (do NOT use floor area alone). List each room separately |
| **Doors, windows & trim** | Count interior/exterior doors; measure linear feet of baseboards, crown, chair rail, soffit, fascia |
| **Existing conditions** | Peeling paint, holes, cracks, water damage, wallpaper, popcorn ceilings, lead paint |
| **Colour scheme & finish** | Multiple colours and high-gloss finishes = more labour and masking time |
| **Special features** | Cabinets, built-ins, stair rails/spindles, accent walls, decorative finishes, decks, fences |

## Cost-Plus Pricing Formula

```
Paintable Area = (wall length × wall height) per room
             × 1.1–1.2 (waste & rough surfaces)

Gallons needed = paintable area ÷ 400 sq ft (interior)
              or ÷ 300 sq ft (rough exterior)

Labour Hours = paintable area ÷ production rate

Item Total = (Labour Hours × Rate) + Materials + Supplies + Equipment

Final Price = Item Total × (1 + Overhead %) × (1 + Profit Margin %)
```

**Example:** 1,500 sq ft house interior (8-ft walls) ≈ 6,000 sq ft wall area.
- Paint (mid-grade, 2 coats): 15 gal × $30 = $450
- Labour: 50 hrs × $40 = $2,000
- Prep: $300 | Supplies: $150 | Overhead (15%): $300
- Cost before profit: $3,200 → with 30% margin → **$4,160** (~$2.77/sq ft)

## Quoting App Integration Notes

### Sub-types

| Sub-type | Key Measurements |
|----------|-----------------|
| Interior Walls & Ceilings | `sqft`, `ceilings`, `doors`, `trim_lf`, `paint_grade`, `surface_condition`, `accent_walls`, `wallpaper_sqft`, `popcorn_sqft` |
| Exterior Siding | `sqft`, `stories`, `trim_lf`, `siding_type`, `ext_doors` |
| Cabinet Refinishing | `doors`, `drawers`, `linear_feet` |
| Deck/Fence/Outbuilding | `deck_sqft`, `fence_lf`, `fence_height`, `outbuilding_sqft` |
| New Construction Interior | `sqft`, `ceilings`, `doors`, `trim_lf`, `paint_grade` |

### Field Reference

- `paint_grade`: 1=builder ($20/gal), 2=mid ($32/gal), 3=premium ($52/gal), 4=designer ($85/gal)
- `surface_condition`: 1=normal, 2=minor repair (+$1–$2/sqft), 3=major repair (+$2–$3/sqft)
- `siding_type`: 1=wood, 2=vinyl, 3=stucco, 4=brick, 5=aluminum — drives specialty coating line items

### Recommended Profit Margins
- **Residential interior**: 45–55%
- **Exterior / specialty coatings**: 50–60%
- **Cabinet refinishing**: 55–65%
- **Commercial**: 50–60%
- **New construction**: 45–55%
